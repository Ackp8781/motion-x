Questions

Short answers to the common ones.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that aren't yet widely downloaded. Click More info → Run anyway. You can check that your download is intact by comparing its SHA-256 with the one shown above.

Where are my projects saved?

In your user folder, at %APPDATA%\Motion X\Projects. That means you can move or update the app folder without losing anything.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no subscription.

Why does AI Enhance need a graphics card?

The neural upscaler runs on your GPU through Vulkan, which almost every NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics card from the last several years supports. Without one, Enhance falls back to a simpler clean-up and resize.

Can I import any video from a link?

Links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Vimeo and many more sites work. Only import videos you made or have permission to use.

How do I uninstall it?

Delete the Motion X folder. To remove your projects too, delete %APPDATA%\Motion X.
