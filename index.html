Animation

Keyframes with real graphs

Animate any property and shape the motion between keyframes with a curve, the way Alight Motion does.

  • Position, scale, rotation, opacity, colour and every effect setting
  • Premade graphs, or drag your own and save them for later
  • Overshoot and bounce by pulling the curve past the top
Drawing

Frame-by-frame animation, over your video

A FlipaClip-style drawing editor built in. Draw straight on top of your footage, frame by frame.

  • Brush, pen, eraser, fill, line and colour picker
  • Onion skin shows the frames before and after
  • Holds, speed from 4 to 30 fps, loop or play once
AI

Rescue low-quality clips

AI Enhance rebuilds detail in blurry, pixelated or blocky video using a neural upscaler on your graphics card. Your footage never leaves your computer.

  • Up to 4× bigger, with a before/after slider
  • Smooth choppy footage up to 240 fps
  • Plus Stabilize, to remove handheld shake

Questions

Short answers to the common ones.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that aren't yet widely downloaded. Click More info → Run anyway. You can check that your download is intact by comparing its SHA-256 with the one shown above.

Where are my projects saved?

In your user folder, at %APPDATA%\Motion X\Projects. That means you can move or update the app folder without losing anything.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no subscription.

Why does AI Enhance need a graphics card?

The neural upscaler runs on your GPU through Vulkan, which almost every NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics card from the last several years supports. Without one, Enhance falls back to a simpler clean-up and resize.

Can I import any video from a link?

Links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Vimeo and many more sites work. Only import videos you made or have permission to use.

How do I update or uninstall it?

To update, download the new zip and replace the old Motion X folder. Your projects stay where they are. To uninstall, delete the folder, and %APPDATA%\Motion X if you also want your projects gone.
